Frequently asked questions

What is planning poker?

Planning poker (also called scrum poker) is an agile estimation technique. Team members estimate story points by privately selecting a card, then reveal together and discuss until they reach consensus.

Is Planning Poker really free?

Yes, it is completely free. There is no signup, no account and no credit card required.

How do I share a room with my team?

Create a room and copy the share link, or have teammates type the room code into the join box on this page.

Can non-voting members join?

Yes. Anyone can join as a spectator (observer) to watch the session without participating in the vote.

Are my rooms private?

Rooms are only reachable by people who have the link or the room code. Empty rooms are cleaned up automatically.

Which point decks are supported?

The default deck (0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, ?, ☕) is enabled by default, with an optional half-points deck and observer mode available in every room.
